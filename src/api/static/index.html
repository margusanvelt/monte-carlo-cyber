Quantitative Risk Assessment

Define your risk using ranges to account for uncertainty. We use the PERT distribution (Min, Most Likely, Max).

1. Scenario Definition

E.g., "Customer Data Breach", "Ransomware Lockdown", "Cloud Outage"
Higher numbers (e.g., 10,000) are more accurate but take longer.

2. Frequency (Events per Year)

How often do you expect this to happen? 0.1 = once every 10 years, 2.0 = twice a year.

3. Magnitude (Financial Impact per Event)

Total cost including forensics, legal, fines, and lost productivity if the event happens.

Simulation Results

Mean Annual Loss (ALE)
The statistically expected average loss per year. Use this for standard budgeting.
P95 - Value at Risk (VaR)
"Worst Case": There is only a 5% chance that losses will exceed this amount in a year.
Median Loss (P50)
The midpoint: 50% of simulated years had a loss lower than this.
